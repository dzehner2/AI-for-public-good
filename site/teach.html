Teacher sign-up opens soon

When it opens, signing up will be a short interview. We'll ask:

  • Which skills you can teach.
  • How proficient you are in each one, from practised to professional or certified instructor.
  • Any credentials or certifications you hold, and who issued them.
  • How you'd like to teach: in person, by video, or in writing.
  • When you're available.

Skills with real safety risk, such as first aid, firearms, chainsaws, diving, boating and riding, will need a current certification before you can teach them here.

Until then, you can tell us you're interested through the contact section on the home page.
