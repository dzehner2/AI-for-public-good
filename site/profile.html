How levels work
  • Rate each skill from "Not yet" to "Can teach". Change it whenever you like.
  • On a course page, tick the learning steps as you do them. When every step is ticked, mark the course complete.
  • Completing a course raises that skill one level, up to "Confident". "Can teach" is your own call.
  • Your overall level goes up every three completed courses.
