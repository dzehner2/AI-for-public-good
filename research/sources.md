# Sources

This file lists **source types** to check and how to weigh them. It deliberately does not
list specific figures; each claim on an idea card must be traced to a dated source recorded
here or in the card's "Sources" section.

## Source types

| Type | Examples of what to look for | Strength | Watch for |
|------|------------------------------|----------|-----------|
| Regulatory filings and permits | Water withdrawal permits, air permits, environmental impact reports, data center siting approvals | High for facts about a specific facility | Self-reported figures; check for updates |
| Utility and agency data | Municipal water loss reports, drinking water quality reports, grid operator data | High | Coverage gaps, reporting lags |
| Public monitoring networks | Regulatory air monitors, stream gauges, groundwater wells | High | Sparse coverage in low-income areas |
| Peer-reviewed research | Validation studies for sensors, models, and methods | High when replicated | Lab conditions vs. field conditions |
| Government and intergovernmental reports | Environmental and water-resource assessments | High for context | Dated baselines |
| Company sustainability reports | Water, energy, and emissions disclosures from data center operators | Medium | Methodology differences, market-based offsets, selective scope |
| Industry and vendor claims | Product performance, efficiency claims | Low until independently tested | Unverified benchmarks |
| Community testimony and local knowledge | Resident observations, tribal and local knowledge | Essential context; not a substitute for measurement | Must be recorded with consent |
| News and investigative reporting | Local coverage of facility impacts and hearings | Useful for leads | Verify against primary records |

## Evaluation checklist

For each source used on a card:

1. Who produced it, and do they gain from the conclusion?
2. What period and geography does it cover?
3. Is the methodology stated, and can the number be reproduced?
4. Does it measure the thing the card claims, or a proxy?
5. Is there independent corroboration?
