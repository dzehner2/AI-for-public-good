# Research Agenda

Open questions to resolve before the matching ideas move from `seed` to `designed`.
Each question names the idea cards it blocks.

## Cross-cutting

1. **What is the real water and electricity footprint of a given AI facility design,
   by cooling type and climate?** Needed for every compute-related idea.
   Blocks: `waste-heat-reuse`, `reclaimed-water-cooling`, `grid-aware-scheduling`.
2. **Which regions have both high AI-facility demand and water stress?** A siting screen.
   Blocks: `reclaimed-water-cooling`, `brownfield-siting`.
3. **What community benefit-sharing arrangements exist for host communities, and what has
   been shown to work?** Blocks: `community-data-trust`.

## Water

4. **What share of treated water is lost to leaks in typical municipal systems, and what
   detection methods have field results?** Blocks: `pipe-leak-detection`.
5. **Which low-cost water quality sensors are validated against lab reference methods?**
   Blocks: `water-quality-sensing`.

## Air

6. **How well do low-cost air sensors agree with reference monitors after calibration, and
   under which conditions does agreement fail?** Blocks: `community-air-sensors`.
7. **Which satellite and aerial methane detection approaches have published validation,
   and what is their detection threshold?** Blocks: `methane-detection`.

## Land

8. **Which land-cover and brownfield datasets are public, current, and at useful resolution?**
   Blocks: `brownfield-siting`, `watershed-participatory-modeling`.
9. **What participatory modeling methods have been used for watershed or soil planning, and
   how were residents involved in practice?** Blocks: `watershed-participatory-modeling`.

## Community

10. **How do residents currently find and understand permit and public-comment information,
    and where do they drop off?** Blocks: `civic-information-assistant`.
11. **What are the accuracy and language-coverage risks of AI summarization in civic
    settings, and what review processes reduce them?** Blocks: `civic-information-assistant`.
12. **What data-cooperative legal structures are available in the target jurisdictions?**
    Blocks: `community-data-trust`.

## How to close a question

Record the answer as a dated note under the relevant source in `sources.md`, with a link or
citation. Then update the blocked idea card's "Open questions" section and, if it changes a
score, its front matter and review log.
