# Rounding out Useful Skills: research notes

The first 100 skills follow the Art of Manliness list "100 Skills Every Man Should Know". To round
out the set, 50 more skills (101 to 150) were added from established programs that teach life skills
to everyone. No skill is labelled by gender. Each new course names its source on its page
(`src` and `srcUrl` in `site/assets/skills/part5.js` and `part6.js`).

## Gaps in the original 100

Home-keeping and textiles (beyond sewing a button), money management, caring for children, sick
people and older relatives, food safety, meal planning, baking and preserving, relationships and
emotional skills (listening, conflict, grief, boundaries), and wellbeing (mindfulness, flexibility).

  [toolkit](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tools/your-money-your-goals/toolkit/)
