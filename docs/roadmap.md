# Roadmap: 90 Days, Water-First

## Desired End State (DES)

> To create an environment where researchers, community groups, and local officials feel
> confident that AI infrastructure proposals can be judged on their real water, air, land,
> and community impacts, because a transparent, scored idea library and a shareable proposal
> exist, enabling them to choose and pilot projects that leave communities better off,
> producing net-positive outcomes from AI buildout.

**Status:** Drafted from the planning interview. To be confirmed by the team (small team;
all members sign off) before Phase 1 begins. See open item O-1.

## Context

- **Purpose:** shareable proposal material (written doc plus live ranked index)
- **Audience:** small team now; external reviewers and partners at the 90-day mark
- **Priority domain:** water
- **Team:** one analyst or data person, one engineer
- **Partners:** none identified yet; identifying them is part of the plan
- **Geography:** not decided; screening happens in Phase 1

## Success at Day 90

1. A written proposal, reviewed by at least one external reviewer, with the ranked index attached.
2. Scores for all water-domain cards calibrated by the team (see REQ-S5).
3. A short-listed pilot choice with a named partner *candidate* and a draft pilot scope.

A signed pilot agreement is a stretch goal, not a Day-90 requirement.

## Phases

### Phase 0: Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

Goal: a stable base to build on.

- Confirm the DES and the requirements in this folder (O-1, O-2)
- Run the first calibration pass: score three water ideas side by side (rubric.md)
- Add the test suite and confirm it passes on the current tooling
- Decide the web index approach (O-3)

**Exit criteria:** DES and requirements signed off by the team; tests passing; calibration notes written.

### Phase 1: Evidence and Screening (Weeks 3–5)

Goal: know which water idea and which geography to pursue.

- Choose the geography, or a shortlist of two (O-4); record the screen in `research/`
- Log at least 10 dated sources for the water ideas in `research/sources.md`
- Close research questions 4 and 5 from `research/agenda.md` (utility loss rates; validated low-cost water sensors)
- Pick the first pilot from pipe-leak detection, water-quality sensing, or reclaimed-water cooling,
  using the scores and the evidence gathered

**Exit criteria:** geography chosen; first pilot chosen with recorded reasons; water cards at
status `researching` or higher.

### Phase 2: Partners and Drafting (Weeks 4–9, overlaps Phase 1)

Goal: find who would run the pilot and draft what we'd show them.

- Identify 3 to 5 candidate partners (utilities, municipal water departments, watershed groups)
- Draft the written proposal: problem, ranked ideas, method, footprint arithmetic, pilot ask
- Build the web index view (REQ-F10, once O-3 is decided)
- Write the pilot plan for the chosen idea, using the card's pilot section

**Exit criteria:** partner candidate list with a first contact made; proposal draft v1 complete; index view working.

### Phase 3: Review and Ready (Weeks 10–12)

Goal: a proposal that survives outside scrutiny.

- Send the draft to at least one external reviewer with domain expertise (O-5)
- Revise the proposal and scores based on feedback; log changes in each card's review log
- Re-run the calibration check and confirm no unexplained score changes
- Final tests and index regeneration

**Exit criteria:** Day-90 success criteria 1–3 met, or a written note on which are not met and why.

| 2    | DES and requirements signed off; tests green |

## Risks to this plan

| Risk | Avoid | Mitigate | Backup |
|------|-------|----------|--------|
| No utility partner responds | Start outreach in Phase 1, not Phase 2 | Use municipal water and watershed groups as alternates | Propose a desk-based screen as the pilot, clearly labeled |
| Water data too coarse for the chosen geography | Screen data availability before choosing geography | Choose a second geography from the shortlist | Scope the pilot to the one utility with usable data |
| Scores seen as arbitrary by reviewers | Calibrate in Phase 0 and show the arithmetic | Publish rubric and reasons per score | Present ranking as a shortlist, not a verdict |
| Team capacity (two people) | Keep scope to water domain | Defer land and community cards to a later plan | Cut the web index before cutting the proposal |
